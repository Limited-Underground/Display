# OG-018K Physical Retryable Rate-Limit Rejection

Date: 2026-08-09

Status: passed for two role-reversed close-bench cycles using production host
components from OpenTrail and OpenGauge. This is host-mediated and reconstructed,
not durable on-device state, physical authentication, or field behavior.

## Purpose and boundary

Complement OG-018J's terminal stale policy with the retryable branch. OpenTrail
filled its real general-rate allowance with four unique valid events and then
processed the physically delivered normative alert as the fifth event. Its real
responder emitted rejected/rate-limited.

OpenGauge reconstructed the exact sent event as in-flight and passed the returned
bytes through authorization, session, replay/correlation, and negative-ACK
policy. Success required `outbox_completed=false`, zero acknowledgements,
`retry_released=true`, `terminal_failure=false`, exactly one queued entry, and
zero in flight.
