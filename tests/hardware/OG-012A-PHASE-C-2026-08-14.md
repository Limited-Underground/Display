# OG-012A Round-Display Phase-C Evidence - 2026-08-14

Status: partial vendor-image write, visual display, and official-factory
rollback evidence on one candidate. This is not a complete vendor baseline,
OpenGauge firmware, peripheral, performance, compatibility, or support result.
Artifact provenance and the private-backup boundary were established in the
[Phase-B record](OG-012A-PHASE-B-2026-08-14.md).

## Scope and preservation boundary

- The operator assigned the connected battery-free USB candidate public-safe
  ID `OG-DISP-001` and confirmed its previously recorded demo was visible
  before the write cycle.
- The operator confirmed the battery-free cold BOOT-strap recovery procedure
  passed before flashing.
- The other candidate is labeled `OG-DISP-002`; it remained disconnected and
  untouched throughout this evidence cycle.
- A passive privacy-safe roster contained exactly one Started Windows
  Port-class `303A:1001` interface. No port name, MAC, USB serial, raw log,
  private backup hash, or private backup path is retained here.
- The original full-flash backup remains private outside Git. It was not used
  or restore-tested in this cycle.

The public-safe labels distinguish the two candidates for later records. They
do not establish a hidden device identity, exact received-board revision, or
support claim.

## Result and next gate

The recovery-first write/rollback checkpoint passed on `OG-DISP-001`, while
`OG-DISP-002` remains the untouched reference. OG-012A remains `planned`, and
both displays remain candidate-only hardware.

The remaining vendor baseline includes touch coordinates/rotation, PMIC data,
IMU behavior, RTC retention, LVGL resource and timing measurements, stability,
power and heat, and any safely prepared Micro SD or audio checks. OpenGauge
synthetic-gauge firmware, fail-visible stale/error screens, paired independence,
and repeat recovery remain later acceptance gates. No vehicle connection or
support claim is authorized by this result.
