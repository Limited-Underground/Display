Date: 2026-08-09

Status: passed for two role-reversed four-leg close-bench sequences using real

## Sequence

Each role delivered the exact alert, received a correlated rate-limit rejection,
released it for retry, proved it was not ready at 25 ms, prepared the same bytes
at 26 ms, committed a second local send, delivered the same alert again, and
processed a next-sequence accepted ACK at 27 ms.

Success required one recorded remote retry, one final acknowledgement, zero
queued/in-flight entries, zero terminal failures, and exact correlation through
the real peer authorization/session/replay/outbox ingress.
